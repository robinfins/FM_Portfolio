About

I'm Robin, a freelance full-stack developer

I got into this by accident, sort of. I completed a communication degree, then later decided to switch to IT, and somewhere in between I ended up freelancing.

These days I split my time between frontend and backend, with a focus on AI and automation. At Roland Revisjon I built a desktop app, used daily by auditors to pull data from Skatteetaten instead of doing it by hand. At Digin I worked on a pipeline that turns images into segmented 3D digital twins. In between, I've built things for myself too, like a fully automated pipeline that generates and uploads YouTube content using AI-written metadata.

Turns out I really enjoy problem-solving.
Give me a slow, manual process and I'll find a way to make it disappear.
